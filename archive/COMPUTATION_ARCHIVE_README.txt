This archive accompanies rewriting_barnette_tutte_berge_fulkerson.ipynb.

Barnette:
- test_barnette_k.cpp: exhaustive reverse-reachability engine for connected
  patches of bounded size.
- barnette_results.txt: complete one/two-face results for 50 generated graphs.
- steps8res.txt: complete results for 30 generated graphs with 22 dual vertices.
- barnette_big.txt, steps8.txt, step10.txt: graph inputs.
- step10_unresolved.txt: six pair-flip traps in the 48-vertex primal example.

Berge–Fulkerson:
- j5_rewrite.cpp and j5res.txt: exhaustive J5 state-space normalization.
- bfr_results_all.txt: random bridgeless cubic state-space logs.
- bip_results.txt: random bipartite cubic state-space logs.

The notebook contains the mathematical definitions and smaller Python checks.
