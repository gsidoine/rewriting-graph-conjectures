# Low-Defect Structure

When all edge multiplicities lie in `{1,2,3}`, every vertex sees either `(2,2,2)` or `(1,2,3)`.  Defective edges therefore form disjoint even cycles alternating 1 and 3.  A defect 4-cycle is always repairable by flipping a matching common to its two multiplicity-3 edges.  Consequently a nonzero one-coordinate local minimum in a simple cubic graph has `Q>=6`.
