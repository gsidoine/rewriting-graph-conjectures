# Universal Descent Theorem

**Verified theorem.** If `Q>2n`, one of six minimum-weight perfect-matching computations returns a strict coordinate descent.

At a vertex the incident multiplicities are nonnegative integers summing to six, so the maximum local squared deviation is 24.  Summing vertices and dividing by two gives `Q<=12n`.  Since `Q` is even, at most `5n` strict updates are needed to enter `Q<=2n`.
