# Conjectures

1. **Global quadratic normalization:** every sextuple of perfect matchings in a bridgeless cubic graph reaches `Q=0` by non-increasing one-coordinate replacements.
2. **Snark low-defect exclusion:** no cyclically 4-edge-connected snark admits a nonzero one-coordinate local minimum at `Q=6`.

Both are stronger than currently proved statements.  The first implies Berge–Fulkerson whenever a starting sextuple exists, which it does by bridgeless cubic perfect-matching existence componentwise.
