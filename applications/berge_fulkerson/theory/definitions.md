# Definitions

Let `G` be a finite loopless bridgeless cubic multigraph and let `(M1,...,M6)` be an ordered sextuple of perfect matchings; repetitions are allowed.  For each edge,

- `m(e)` is its multiplicity among the six matchings;
- `d(e)=m(e)-2`;
- `Q=sum_e d(e)^2`.

`Q=0` is exactly a Fulkerson cover.  The local neighborhood replaces one coordinate by any perfect matching.  Theorems are unchanged if states are quotient by coordinate permutations into multisets.
