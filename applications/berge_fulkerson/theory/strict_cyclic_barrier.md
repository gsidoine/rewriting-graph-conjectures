# Strict Cyclic Barrier

For a connected nonbipartite cyclically 4-edge-connected cubic graph, `x=1/3` lies in the relative interior of the perfect-matching polytope.  If equality `S_i=n/3` held for any coordinate, the integral odd weight `w_i` would be constant on the polytope.  The vertex-equation representation and `(3I+A)alpha=1` then force `w_i(e)=1/3`, impossible.

Therefore `S_i<n/3` for every coordinate, yielding the residue-dependent bounds in the paper.  This per-coordinate argument is the repair made during audit.
