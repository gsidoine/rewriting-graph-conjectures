# Exact Defect Identity

For a nontrivial colouring `V(H)=A union B`, define `psi(F)=c(F)-1+beta(F)` and `Phi=psi(H[A])+psi(H[B])`.  Bichromatic dual edges correspond to a primal even subgraph `X`, a disjoint union of cycles.  If `X` omits `z` primal vertices and has `t` cycles, Euler counting and the Jordan curve theorem give `Phi=z+2(t-1)`.
