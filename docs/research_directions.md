# Forward program — theory and applications

**Theory track**: establish rigorous connections among direct-repair width, strict and neutral rewrite distance, hypergraph transversal and separator-width decomposition, emphasizing **bounded graph degree and bounded numbers of coordinates** to avoid trivial universality caused by scaling degree. Develop finite-state cut interfaces that encode accessibility, not only existence of compatible certificates. Target an independent theorem with implications in at least two certificate types.

**Application track**: use the theory to examine one or two externally motivated open problems, presently (i) cubic matching-cover reconfiguration and (ii) edge-connectivity partitioning under 6-edge-connectivity, while tracking updated problem status (notably recent 3-decomposition preprints). Define up-front falsification tests and a stop/pivot criterion.

**Proof reliability**: check every new theorem against the nearest known literature, preserve explicit counterexamples, distinguish heuristic generators from small checkers, and pursue Lean formalization only once a reusable formal result is specified.
