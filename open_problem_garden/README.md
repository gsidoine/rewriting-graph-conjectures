# Open Problem Garden Research Pass

The handover requested a fresh attack on graph-theory problems rated at least two or three stars.  The pass was conducted in July 2026 and kept separate from the three manuscript audits so exploratory observations could not leak into theorem claims.

The final target remains the three-star **3-Decomposition Conjecture**.  The programme did not solve it, but it produced and audited two directly relevant results: a counterexample to a natural fixed-cycle lemma and an NP-completeness theorem showing that fixed-cycle completion is algorithmically hard under strong promises.

A separate finite check was performed on the three-star **3-Edge-Coloring Conjecture**: all 25 three-edge-colourable connected simple cubic graph classes through ten vertices in the supplied catalogue have a removable edge.  This is finite evidence only.
